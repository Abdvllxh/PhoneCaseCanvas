Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

Phone Case Canvas ("we", "us", or "our") operates custom phone case vending machines across the Greater Toronto Area, Ontario, Canada. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in accordance with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use our website contact form or interact with our machines:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • Photos you upload to our machines to create a custom case
  • Messages or inquiries you send us

Contact form submissions are processed via Web3Forms. See their privacy policy at web3forms.com/privacy.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To process and fulfil your custom case order at the machine
  • To improve our products and services
  • To send relevant updates if you have opted in

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.

4. Photos You Upload

Photos uploaded at our vending machines are used solely to print your custom phone case. They are not stored on our servers after your order is complete and are never shared with third parties.

5. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months for customer service purposes, then securely deleted. Machine transaction data is retained for 90 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. To make a request, contact us at vendingphonecasecanvas@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out at vendingphonecasecanvas@gmail.com or call (289) 514-2661.

We accept credit cards, debit cards, and QR code payments at our machines. All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD) and include applicable taxes. Payment is required before a case is printed.

Accessibility

Last updated: June 2026

Our Commitment

Phone Case Canvas is committed to ensuring our website and services are accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

Website Accessibility Features

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper heading hierarchy
  • All images include descriptive alt text
  • Keyboard navigation supported throughout the site
  • Sufficient colour contrast in both light and dark modes
  • Focus indicators visible for all interactive elements
  • Forms include clearly labelled fields and error messages
  • Animations respect the prefers-reduced-motion setting

Physical Machine Accessibility

We are working to ensure our vending machines are placed at accessible heights and locations. If you encounter a machine that is not accessible to you, please let us know and we will do our best to assist.

Ontario AODA Compliance

We are committed to meeting the requirements of the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and the Integrated Accessibility Standards Regulation (IASR).

Known Limitations

We are continually working to improve accessibility. If you experience any barriers, please contact us directly and we will respond within 2 business days.
